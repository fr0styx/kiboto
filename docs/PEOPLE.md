# People

**Settings → People** lists everyone who can use this Kiboto. Each person has their own
page: who they are, what they can use, how they sign in, and their data.

## Adding a person

**Add a person** takes three steps:

1. **Who**: their name and role.
2. **What they can use**: the shared folders they may open, sign-in hours, and more.
3. **How they get in**: either **create the account now** (you choose a sign-in name and a
   first password of 8 characters or more), or **send an invite link** (they choose their
   own sign-in name and password; the link works once, for the days you set). Some roles
   can only be created directly.

After adding someone, **Add another like {name}** starts the next person with the same
choices, which is quick for several kids.

## Roles

| Role | What they get |
|---|---|
| Administrator | Everything: people, settings, and every private folder |
| Family member | Their own folder and the shared folders you choose |
| Kid | Their own folder, the folders you choose, and limits |
| Guest | Can look at the shared folders you choose |

Under **More roles**: App manager, Storage admin, Backup manager and Viewer, for someone who
helps run one part of the NAS without being an administrator.

## A person's page

- **What they can use**: their folders, a storage limit, and **limits** such as sign-in
  hours.
- **How they sign in**: whether two-step sign-in is on, **set a new password**, and **where
  they are signed in**.
- **Lost phone or tablet?**: sign them out on every device and, if their two-step code was
  on that phone, turn two-step sign-in off so they can set it up on a new one (your own
  password confirms it). They are told.
- **Apps away from home**: a one-time link that signs them in to the family sign-in for
  apps, to add a passkey there.
- **Their data**: download or erase what Kiboto keeps about them (Kib chats, what search learned from
  their files); erasing leaves their files and account in place.
- **Turn off or remove**: turn the account off, or remove it and choose what happens to
  their files: keep them, give them to someone else, or delete them too (which can't be
  undone).

## Household rules

**Household rules** sets whether adults must use two-step sign-in (off, everyone but kids and
guests, or chosen roles) and shows who has not set it up yet. People with two-step sign-in
use a separate password for network folders, which they choose when they turn it on.
