# Notifications

**Settings → Notifications** starts with one sentence: where alerts go, or that nobody is told
yet when something breaks. Choose where they go below it.

## Where alerts go

**Set up** a channel: e-mail (SMTP), Telegram (a bot token and a chat ID), Discord (a channel
webhook), ntfy (a server and topic), or under **More services** a webhook (JSON) or Apprise,
which reaches more than a hundred other services. A channel whose last delivery failed says
so.

**Write alerts in** sets the one language the household's alerts are written in (English until
you choose).

## What you are told about

Alerts come for backups, disk health, storage pools, temperatures, fans, copies and imports,
apps, and everything else, each from a level you choose: everything, warnings and up, or
only critical. A weekly health report can arrive on Sunday mornings.

**How often**: each alert as it happens, or one message a day at a time you pick. Critical
alerts still go out at once.

**Fine-tune** holds the temperature limits and how long Kiboto waits before repeating an
alert about the same thing; a more serious alert about it still comes at once.

## My alerts

**My alerts** are just for you: your own e-mail or ntfy topic, about the things you care
about, at your own level. Every person can set their own.

## The bell

Inside Kiboto, the bell in the top bar shows what needs a look; **See all activity** opens
the full history.
